Our Story

Built on Expertise,
Driven by Purpose

AC Joyo Consultancy and Technical Services Inc. was founded with a single mission: to make world-class consultancy and technology accessible to businesses of all sizes — especially those in the regions.

Over the years, the company has grown into a trusted partner for government units, schools, SMEs, and enterprises alike, while expanding into complementary ventures under the Joyo Group umbrella.

2014

Company Founded

AC Joyo Consultancy registered and launched its first technical consulting engagements.

2017

Joyo Imprints Launched

Expanded into commercial printing services, serving corporate and institutional clients.

2019

AppGenStore & Joyo Tech

Ventured into technology retail and digital product development, broadening the group's reach.

2022

Coffee Shop Opens

The Joyo Coffee Shop opened its doors, completing the group's diverse portfolio of ventures.
